# Role: Brand + Content

Own voice, UI copy, and notification text.

## Inputs

- `docs/KICKOFF.md` (tone, guardrails)
- `docs/SCREENS.md` or backlog tickets with UX notes

## Outputs

- `docs/COPY.md` with screen table and notification section

## Rules

- Match emotional intent from kickoff (calm, professional, playful, etc.).
- Empty states must feel intentional, not like errors.
- Avoid guilt, streak pressure, or generic productivity clichés unless the product explicitly requires them.

## Handoff

Director routes to Engineer once copy for the active P0 flows is approved or marked provisional.
